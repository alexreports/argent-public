Argent Public publishes from the **public record** only. Our work can be verified
by anyone, line by line.

## Our principles

1. **Everything is sourced.** Every factual claim links to a public document —
   a decree, contract, report, financial statement, or official release.
2. **Everything is archived.** For each source we provide a *live link* and an
   *archived copy* (Wayback Machine and/or archive.today), so the evidence
   survives even if the original page disappears.
3. **No speculation.** We document what is. Where the record is incomplete, we
   say so explicitly rather than fill the gaps.
4. **Facts kept separate from analysis.** Figures and quotations are presented
   as they stand. Any added context is clearly marked.
5. **Public corrections.** Any error is corrected, dated, and explained in the
   [corrections log]({{ '/en/corrections/' | relative_url }}).

## How to read a dossier

Every dossier follows the same four-part structure:

- **The question** — what we set out to establish.
- **The evidence** — the public documents that answer it.
- **The cost** — what it means for public funds.
- **The change sought** — the decision or accountability expected.

## Privacy

This site uses **no analytics, no cookies, and collects no data**. The only way
to follow us is the [RSS feed]({{ '/en/follow/' | relative_url }}) — no email,
no account.
