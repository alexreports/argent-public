## What is RSS?

RSS is an open format that delivers our new publications to the app of your
choice **without giving us any information about you**. You stay in control; we
don't even know you've subscribed.

## How to subscribe

1. Install an RSS reader (for example: NetNewsWire, Feedly, Inoreader,
   Thunderbird, or a browser extension).
2. Copy the feed address above.
3. Paste it into your reader. New dossiers, explainers, and record entries will
   appear automatically.
