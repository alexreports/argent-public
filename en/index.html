The question of the moment

The Green Ledger: natural gas against the transition targets

Quebec banned fracking on its own territory, yet its gas network is still supplied 95–97 % by imported shale gas. At the same time, the sole distributor misses its regulatory renewable natural gas targets by more than 70 %. Our Green Ledger series documents this gap between stated commitments and the public record: importing fracking, exiting a fossil pipeline at a profit, and the quota gap.
