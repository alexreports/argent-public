The question of the moment

The Cost You Cannot See: why large public projects keep failing in the same ways

Quebec's large public technology projects are failing repeatedly, in the same patterns, across different governments. This is no longer a claim made by critics — it is the written conclusion of the province's own oversight bodies. In 2026, two major reports (from the Autorité des marchés publics and the Commission Gallant) documented the same structural gaps. Four months before a general election, our series The Cost You Cannot See puts these questions on the public record.
